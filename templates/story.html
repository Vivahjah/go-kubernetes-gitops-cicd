Our story

Kiln & Co. started in a shared studio with one wheel and a second-hand kiln. We wanted to make plates and mugs people reach for every morning, not just admire on a shelf.

Today we still work in small batches. Each piece is shaped by hand, glazed in colours taken from clay, moss and coastline, and fired until it is tough enough for daily life.
